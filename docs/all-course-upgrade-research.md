
# All-Course Upgrade Research Notes

## Verified official source hubs

- MIT OpenCourseWare: https://ocw.mit.edu/ — official free course materials including lecture notes, exams, and videos.
- MIT Missing Semester: https://missing.csail.mit.edu/ — official tool-proficiency course with shell, development environment, debugging/profiling, Git, packaging, code quality, and YouTube lecture playlists.
- UC Berkeley CS61C: https://cs61c.org/ — official machine-structures course with C, pointers, arrays, memory, RISC-V, labs, projects, and linked lecture/video resources.
- British Council LearnEnglish: https://learnenglish.britishcouncil.org/ — official level-structured listening, reading, writing, speaking, grammar, vocabulary, business English, and interactive exercises.

## Curriculum implications

The all-course upgrade should distinguish lecture embeds from official course/source hubs. MIT and Berkeley pages may expose recordings through a course platform or linked playlist rather than permitting direct iframe embedding; these should render as explicit source-studio fallbacks rather than blank frames. Skill courses should use level-based practice and primary institutional resources. Technical routes should progress from foundations and tooling into systems boundaries, measurement, testing, and capstone evidence instead of claiming mastery from passive video completion alone.
