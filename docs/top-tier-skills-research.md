# Top-Tier Skills Curriculum Research

This research log records only source material reviewed for the nine-course expansion. It is not learner-facing copy; it preserves provenance for catalogue decisions.

## 1. English for Technical Work

| Source | Verified material | Planned Rampage use | URL |
|---|---|---|---|
| British Council LearnEnglish | Free material organised by English level and by listening, reading, writing, speaking, grammar, vocabulary, and business English. | Establish a learner’s baseline, then assign one deliberate listening, speaking, writing, and vocabulary loop per phase. | https://learnenglish.britishcouncil.org/free-resources |
| BBC Learning English: Work English | Office-English episodes, job-application language, work vocabulary, and communication material oriented to workplace contexts. | Short listening/transcript exercises for meetings, status updates, job language, and respectful disagreement. | https://www.bbc.co.uk/learningenglish/business-english |
| Google Technical Writing | Technical Writing courses, resources, accessibility objectives, and helpful-error-message material; aimed at engineers and adjacent roles. | Use source-led documentation rewrites and concise-explanation practice after foundational English work. | https://developers.google.com/tech-writing |

### Design decision

The course will be **English for Technical Work**, not a generic English syllabus. Its beginner path starts with clear work-English input and produces a written status update, a short spoken walkthrough, and a documentation rewrite. The Google material is presented as an advanced applied-writing branch only, because its own guidance assumes some English proficiency and is targeted at engineers or engineering-adjacent roles.

|---|---|---|---|
| MIT EECS Communication Lab CommKit | Short scientific and technical communication guides, including elevator pitches, email, professional bios, slides, virtual presentations, and application material. The collection includes annotated examples and is shared under CC BY-NC 4.0 unless otherwise noted. | Build short practice loops: audience map, two-minute explanation, concise email, professional bio, and a slide walk-through. | https://mitcommlab.mit.edu/eecs/use-the-commkit/ |
| Open University OpenLearn | A free workplace-communication course covering verbal, non-verbal, written, challenging, diverse, and future-work communication, with reflection and goal-setting activities. | Provide beginner reflection prompts, listening exercises, remote-work scenarios, and a personal communication plan. | https://www.open.edu/openlearn/money-business/effective-communication-the-workplace |

### Design decision

Two adjacent courses will remain distinct: **Clear Communication** focuses on message structure, listening, and high-signal writing; **Professional Presence** focuses on preparation, reflection, professional bios, presentations, and respectful conduct. Neither course will promise personality transformation or use pseudo-scientific personality scoring.

## 3. Critical Thinking and Decision Systems

| Source | Verified material | Planned Rampage use | URL |
|---|---|---|---|
| Harvard Professional & Lifelong Learning | Introductory, self-paced argument course with free audit access; covers argument mapping, assumptions, biases, respectful disagreement, and listening. | Convert a claim into an argument map, identify assumptions, and write a fair counterargument. | https://pll.harvard.edu/course/structure-and-function-argument-introduction-critical-thinking |
| Carnegie Mellon Open Learning Initiative | Open and free evidence-based-management material addressing what counts as evidence, evidence quality, hidden claims/assumptions, problem clarity, and preferred solutions. | Build decision briefs that separate an assumed problem, available evidence, uncertainty, alternatives, and next experiment. | https://oli.cmu.edu/courses/evidence-based-management-o-f/ |

### Design decision

**Critical Thinking** will teach argument mapping and evidence inspection. **Decision Systems** will turn those skills into a short decision brief, with explicit uncertainty and an evidence-quality check rather than generic productivity advice.

| Source | Verified material | Planned Rampage use | URL |
|---|---|---|---|
| Google Technical Writing | Technical Writing resources for engineers, computer-science students, and engineering-adjacent professionals; the site provides course objectives, pre-class material, accessibility guidance, and error-message writing resources. | Turn vague writing habits into a documentation route: active voice, structure, procedures, accessibility, and helpful error messages. | https://developers.google.com/tech-writing |
| Carnegie Mellon Open Learning Initiative | An eight-module, open and free Learning to Learn Online course covering self-directed learning, plan-monitor-evaluate, information management, teamwork, time management, feedback, strategic reading, and reflection. | Build an evidence-based learning-system route: study plan, weekly review, source-reading workflow, and learning retrospective. | https://oli.cmu.edu/courses/learning-to-learn-online-o-f/ |

### Design decision

**Applied Writing** is a deliberate-practice course whose proof of work is a concise technical guide and a rewritten unclear message. **Learning Systems** uses plan-monitor-evaluate and strategic reading to produce a learner-owned operating system—not claims of guaranteed productivity or accelerated mastery.

### Verified source decisions

| Course | Verified source and scope | Design use |
|---|---|---|
| Career Navigation | OpenLearn’s Career Ready collection offers free material around workplace communication, job applications, interviews, personal branding, career resilience, and understanding a sector. | Create a beginner path that culminates in an evidence-based role map, CV/application plan, and interview story bank. |
| Negotiation | The University of Michigan’s *Successful Negotiation* describes a 12-hour beginner-level course with six modules that cover preparation, tactics, closing, performance/evaluation, and practice. It is hosted through Coursera/edX; general public access and certificate terms are platform-dependent, so Rampage will treat it as an outbound primary source, not claim it is freely available. | Teach a safe, ethical preparation brief with interests, alternatives, boundaries, questions, and reflection. Do not offer legal/contract advice. |
| Collaborative Problem-Solving | CMU ConflictU is an introductory conflict-resolution resource designed around team-project situations, interests/needs, and constructive disagreement. Its public page identifies an access/contact flow for course use; Rampage will link it as a reviewed supporting resource rather than represent it as an unrestricted embed. | Build a collaboration route with team agreements, meeting decisions, disagreement framing, and a post-mortem. |

### Course-design constraint

Negotiation and collaboration will focus on preparation, communication, ethical framing, and reflection. They will not provide legal, financial, employment, or contract-specific advice, and their external source availability will be displayed accurately in the course metadata.

## 6. Final responsive catalogue review — 2026-08-18

The dedicated `/courses` route was reviewed at desktop and narrow phone widths. On wide screens, the editorial hero, course-map count, filter strip, and route-dossier cards maintain a clear discovery hierarchy. On phones, the catalogue collapses into a single column with retained source labels, route actions, and bottom-navigation clearance. The content density intentionally remains high: each dossier preserves its course route, source cue, duration estimate, and proof-of-work signal rather than becoming a generic card feed.
