# BlackVault Rampage — Design Ground Truth

This is a reference-led build. The supplied BlackVault branding is the ground-truth visual spec: midnight navy field, cream type, orange directional marks, and an acid-lime starburst symbol. Fidelity to this identity takes priority over generic dashboard conventions.

## Reference Spec

The interface should feel like an elite technical academy rather than a conventional LMS. Use a dark navy canvas, oversized cream editorial typography, orange as the action color, and acid-lime as the signal color. Preserve the sharp angular language from the supplied Rampage mark and the starburst symbol from the BlackVault logo.

## Design Movement

Swiss International Typographic Style crossed with technical field manuals and late-90s systems diagrams.

## Core Principles

1. **Signal over decoration:** every accent is a navigational or learning signal.
2. **Editorial hierarchy:** large, assertive headlines and compact metadata create a deliberate reading path.
3. **Angular momentum:** clipped corners, directional bars, ruled lines, and stepped geometry echo the Rampage mark.
4. **Earned progress:** the interface makes the learner's path visible and demanding without gamifying the content into noise.

## Color Philosophy

Midnight navy is the long-focus environment; it reduces visual fatigue and gives the identity its confidence. Warm cream is the reading surface and keeps the page human. Orange is reserved for action, movement, and proof of work. Acid-lime identifies a live signal, completion, or featured route. Black is used for icon contrast and technical annotations.

## Layout Paradigm

Use an asymmetric split composition: an anchored left rail for orientation, a wide editorial content field, and occasional right-side utility panels. Avoid a centered marketing stack. Long pages should feel like a map unfolding across a workbench, with horizontal rules and offset content blocks creating rhythm.

## Signature Elements

- Acid-lime starburst badge used for the BlackVault mark, active states, and completion signals.
- Orange directional slash bars and clipped modules used as section markers and CTA edges.
- Fine cream rules, monospace labels, and compact system annotations that make the academy feel instrumented.

## Interaction Philosophy

Interactions should feel like operating a serious instrument: immediate, crisp, and legible. Hover states brighten the relevant signal; completed lessons persist locally and visibly; dialogs and menus remain compact and purposeful. No interaction should rely on color alone.

## Animation

Use short 160–240ms transitions with a strong ease-out. On first load, reveal the hero rail, then the headline, then the course map in 50ms staggered steps. Keep progress fills and active markers smooth. Respect `prefers-reduced-motion` and never animate layout dimensions for primary navigation.

## Typography System

Display: **Space Grotesk**, 600–700, for the Rampage wordmark, hero headline, and section titles. Body: **DM Sans**, 400–500, for reading and controls. Technical metadata: **JetBrains Mono**, 500–700, in uppercase with generous tracking. Headline hierarchy is intentionally oversized; body copy stays narrow and readable.

## Brand Essence

**BlackVault Rampage is a demanding, privacy-first systems academy for builders who want elite fundamentals, taught through real labs and hard projects.** Personality: exacting, direct, energizing.

## Brand Voice

Headlines are short and declarative. CTAs are verbs with consequence. Microcopy is specific, never inflated.

- “This is hard. That’s why it matters.”
- “Build the machine. Then understand it.”

## Wordmark & Logo

Use the supplied BlackVault symbol as a visible mark, paired with a custom-styled text lockup rather than a default wordmark. The Rampage name should use a wide, low-contrast geometric treatment with an orange slash accent.

## Signature Brand Color

**BlackVault Lime — #B6FF5C**. It is the live-signal color: unmistakable, optimistic, and used sparingly enough to retain authority.
